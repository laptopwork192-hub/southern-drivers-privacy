Privacy Policy

In short:
  • No account and no sign-in. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • No ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The game does not collect, send, sell or share any information about you.
  • Your progress stays on your phone.
  • Southern Drivers is for players aged 13 and over.

Who we are

Southern Drivers is a driving game for Android made by the independent developer tejas ("we", "us"), based in India. This policy explains what happens to information when you use the game. It applies to the Southern Drivers app on Google Play.

Information we collect

None. The game has no sign-in and never asks for your name, email address, phone number, age or other details. It contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no third-party tracking software. We do not run any servers, and the game does not send information about you or how you play to us or to anyone else.

Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game saves on your own phone:

  • game progress and settings (coins, unlocked vehicles and upgrades, records and statistics, sound and control options).

This stays in the game's private storage on your phone and we cannot see it. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data in your phone's settings. If your phone's own Android backup is turned on, Google may include the game's saved progress in your personal device backup, under your Google account and Google's policies.

Permissions

  • Vibration – for crash and bump feedback (can be turned off in the game's Settings).

The game does not use your camera, microphone, location, contacts, phone calls, messages or files.

Children

Southern Drivers is intended for players aged 13 and over. The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 or the EU/UK GDPR), you may have rights over your personal data. Because the game collects none and we hold none, the only data is what is saved on your phone, which you can delete at any time by clearing the game's data or uninstalling it. You can also contact us with any question.

Changes to this policy

If a future update changes what the game does with information (for example by adding ads), we will update this policy and the date at the top before that update is released, and tell you in the game.
